About Kam

I am Kamal Bramwell (Kam), a Software Architect and Senior Mobile Engineer with a passion for elegant cross-platform engineering. My core focus lies in designing scalable architectures, pioneering zero-dependency robust solutions, and rapidly shipping high-quality products.

With extensive experience in Kotlin Multiplatform, Jetpack Compose, and SwiftUI, I bridge the gap between platforms while maintaining strict native performance. I advocate for Trunk-Based Development, comprehensive Test-Driven Development (with manual test doubles), and End-to-End Encryption cryptographic protocol design.
