Core Invariants

Engineered for solitude. Built for permanence.

Every design choice in Timeline strips away the attention-economy toxicity of modern feeds, leaving you with an intimate, frictionless canvas for personal reflection.

INVARIANT // 01

Solo Social Stream

Reverse-chronological capture with zero vanity metrics. No public counters, no followers, no broadcast pressures. All the familiar speed and fluid gesture ergonomics of modern microblogging, without the audience.

No algorithms • No likes • Chronological
INVARIANT // 02

Text, Photos & Voice Memos

Rich multi-format micro-journaling made instant. Attach spontaneous snapshots and one-tap voice memos with minimal, lightweight playback pills. Organize thoughts naturally using inline #hashtags.

Voice waveforms • High-res photos • Tags
INVARIANT // 03

Biometric Vault & Tap-to-Reveal

Complete shoulder-surfing obscurity. Secure your journal with Face ID, Touch ID, or Biometric fingerprint unlock. Tap-to-reveal content masking keeps sensitive entries blurred until you intentionally tap to inspect them.

Face ID • Fingerprint • Anti-shoulder surfing
INVARIANT // 04

100% Local-First & Zero Leaks

Stored strictly on your sandboxed device SQLite database. Zero external servers, zero telemetry SDKs, zero analytics trackers, and zero third-party cloud synchronization. Your journal never touches our hardware because we operate no servers.

Sandboxed SQLite • Zero telemetry • Offline
